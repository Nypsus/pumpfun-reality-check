03Why "buying early" is not an edge

The pump.fun launch price is not scarce information. It is a constant, readable on-chain in about 200 ms (30 virtual SOL against 1.073 × 10¹⁵ virtual tokens).

So you can enter at ×1.00 — and we actually do: 54% of measured entries landed at the exact launch price. That is not skill. It is easy precisely because nobody wants it. The launch price is cheap for a reason.
